# Aimprove Design Direction (Waterllama-Inspired)

Reading this as: Mobile-first playful, tactile learning tracker for Japanese (WaniKani SRS 1-60) and Quran Hafalan (604-page Madani Mushaf), styled with Waterllama's joyful, squircle, pastel-pop aesthetic, dial ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2.

## Identity & Mood
- **Concept**: Playful, joyful daily learning companion inspired by Waterllama. Whimsical yet focused study tracking with bubbly squircles, juicy pastel colors, tactile pill buttons, and a central daily "Knowledge Vessel" progress tracker that fills up as you complete reviews and Mushaf pages.
- **Dials**: `ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2`
  - **ENERGY 2 (Balanced & Joyful)**: Friendly, cheerful, inviting colors that encourage daily habit formation without overwhelming visual clutter.
  - **RHYTHM 2 (Varied & Tactile)**: Central daily completion vessel hero card, paired with chunky quick-action pill cards, playful stat bubbles, and clean reader surfaces.
  - **MOTION 2 (Tactile & Springy)**: Satisfying button press feedback (`scale: 0.96` on active press), smooth progress fill transitions, responsive badge updates.

## Visual Design Elements (Waterllama Signature)
1. **Chunky Squircles & Pill Shapes**: Cards use generous `24px-28px` corner radii; action buttons and tags use full pill shapes (`999px`).
2. **Central Daily Goal Vessel**: A prominent circular/pill visual fill gauge at the top of the dashboard tracking combined daily progress (Reviews + Sabaq/Sabqi + Lessons) like Waterllama's water intake character/meter.
3. **Pastel-Pop Color System**:
   - Soft, warm creamy background with high-contrast surfaces.
   - Japanese Domain:
     - Radical: Sky Cyan (`#0284C7`, bg `#E0F2FE`)
     - Kanji: Coral Watermelon (`#F43F5E`, bg `#FFE4E6`)
     - Vocab: Grape Berry (`#8B5CF6`, bg `#EDE9FE`)
     - Grammar: Sun Amber (`#F59E0B`, bg `#FEF3C7`)
   - Quran Hafalan Domain:
     - Sabaq (New): Fresh Mint Emerald (`#10B981`, bg `#D1FAE5`)
     - Sabqi (Recent): Warm Honey Gold (`#F59E0B`, bg `#FEF3C7`)
     - Manzil (FSRS): Lagoon Teal (`#06B6D4`, bg `#CFFAFE`)
4. **Juicy Typography & Badges**:
   - High-contrast, bold numbers with rounded friendly styling.
   - Clear icon pills with playful visual tags.
