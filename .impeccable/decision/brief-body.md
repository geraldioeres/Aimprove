## Direction contract

THESIS: Aimprove is a daily attendance card. Every study day earns a stamp, like a rajio taiso card or a pesantren setoran card. This refuses the gamified mascot-and-XP language-app layout and the plain white list dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: White card stock on a faint warm-grey desk (#F3F2EE), never cream. Ink #16161A. 2px ink rules, 6px corners. Red hanko #E5402A for stamps and streaks only. Indigo #25348C is the primary action ink. Marigold #F2C230 marks Lessons. Item types are solid fills (radical #1F7AE0, kanji #E0338A, vocab #7B3FD6, grammar #0E8A6E); Quran stages are sabaq #1F8A4C, sabqi #C26A00, manzil #25348C. Zen Kaku Gothic New is the voice. Pressable buttons have a 2px ink outline and a 3px offset ink base that drops on press.

STORY: Open the app and see this week's card. Today's box is empty until the goal is met. Do Reviews or Lessons, tick off each Quran setoran, and watch today's box get stamped. Come back tomorrow.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header "Today" plus the week label. Then two pressable count buttons (Reviews in indigo, Lessons in marigold). Then the "Setoran today" checklist of Quran pages with tick boxes.
